The drift problem なぜ自動化か

Fig. 00·A — Motivation
sys_users従業員マスタ · 95 rows
＋
cmn_department部門マスタ · 20 rows
→
Excel, by hand年に一度 手作業で作図

Every reorganization is redrawn manually — so the chart drifts: it holds people who left the master (芹澤 · 岡本 · 河合), kanji variants (田崎 vs 田﨑), and a phantom department (ソリューション営業部). This app makes the master the single source of truth and surfaces the drift instead of hiding it.

What it does 機能一覧

Fig. 00·B — Capabilities
01

自動生成

Auto-generation

Joins the two masters, builds the tree, orders every roster by rank, and renders one printable chart.

02

階層と序列

Hierarchy & rank

Tree from Parent → Name; nine ranks 代表取締役 → 課員 drive the order — never from Manager.

03

兼務モデル

Concurrent duties

A separate assignment relation lets one person hold posts in many departments — shown with the red 兼 seal.

04

保守と履歴

Maintenance & history

Edit employees & departments through a UI; every change is written to an append-only audit trail.

05

名前の識別

Name disambiguation

Last-name-only, with 佐藤(悠) / 佐藤(晃) collisions resolved and location tags like 大西【大阪】.

06

印刷対応

Print & PDF

UTF-8, CJK-safe, A3 landscape. Interactive on screen; one click to a print-ready PDF.
