EXCEL, BY HAND年に一度 手作業

Redrawn by hand every reorg, so the chart drifts — it keeps people who left the master (芹澤 · 岡本 · 河合), kanji variants (田崎 / 田﨑), a phantom department (ソリューション営業部). This app makes the master the single source of truth and surfaces the drift instead of hiding it.
